# Mawa Shipping website content plan

## Goal
Transform the current logistics homepage into a focused Mawa Shipping website using the supplied copy, Mawa Group positioning, and Bangladesh port-agency priorities.

## Page structure
- Replace Northstar branding, navigation, contact details, calls to action, and footer content with Mawa Shipping.
- Rework the opening area with the licensing statement, Singapore and Bangladesh operations contacts, trust signals, and port status panel.
- Replace the existing company content with the four-part Mawa Group infrastructure advantage.
- Turn the service carousel into a six-category interactive service matrix with selectable tabs and complete service lists.
- Add dedicated Singapore desk, transshipment, and Bangladesh ports coverage sections.
- Replace the current quote area with the full PDA request form and all supplied vessel, port, service, and contact fields.
- Keep the customer showcase and testimonials only where they support the new company narrative.

## Interaction and presentation
- Preserve the sharp Archivo typography, responsive layout, restrained motion, and current maritime visual direction.
- Make the service categories easy to switch on desktop and mobile.
- Make the PDA form functional in-page, with a clear completion state after submission.
- Use obvious placeholders for the phone numbers and Singapore address that were not supplied.

## Verification
- Check all navigation targets, service tabs, form controls, and mobile menu.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts for clipping and overlap.
- Update page title, descriptions, and social-sharing metadata for Mawa Shipping.
